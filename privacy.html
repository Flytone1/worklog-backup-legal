Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11.

WorkLog Backup requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which lets the app see and manage only the files it creates itself in the owner's own Google Drive.

The app is used by its owner only. It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

Backup files are encrypted before upload, and the decryption key never leaves the owner's computer.

No data is retained by the developer other than the backup files in the owner's own Google Drive.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: via the developer's GitHub account.
